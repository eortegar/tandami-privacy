Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Tandami respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Tandami mobile application.

Information stored on your device

Tandami does not require you to create an account. Information you enter to use the app, such as family member names, avatars, turn configurations, schedules, preferences, and turn history, is stored locally on your device.

Tandami does not transmit this family and turn information to Tandami-operated servers.

If you uninstall the app or clear its local data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted unless it is preserved by a device or platform backup mechanism.

In-app purchases

Tandami offers an optional one-time purchase called Tandami Unlimited.

Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play. Tandami also uses RevenueCat to manage purchase validation and access to purchased features.

RevenueCat may process technical and transaction information necessary to provide these services, including device and operating-system information, app usage timestamps related to its service, Google Play purchase tokens, transaction information, and purchase history.

Tandami does not receive or store your payment card details.

How information is used

Purchase-related and technical information processed through RevenueCat is used to provide app functionality, validate purchases, maintain access to Tandami Unlimited, restore purchases, prevent purchase fraud, and provide purchase-related analytics.

Sharing of information

Tandami does not sell your personal information.

Purchase-related information is processed by service providers required to operate in-app purchases, including Google Play and RevenueCat, subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Tandami does not use your family member names, turn history, schedules, or other locally stored family information for advertising.

Advertising

Tandami does not display third-party advertising.

Data security

Family and turn information is stored locally on your device and is protected by the security mechanisms provided by your device and operating system.

Data transmitted to third-party purchase services is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Family and turn information stored by Tandami remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to any device or platform backup mechanisms.

Purchase records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as necessary to process purchases, restore entitlements, prevent fraud, maintain transaction records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

If you have a privacy or data-deletion request relating to purchase information associated with Tandami, contact us at the email address below.

Children's privacy

Tandami is designed to help parents and families manage everyday turns. Tandami does not require children to create accounts and does not intentionally collect family member information entered into the app on Tandami-operated servers.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Tandami's features, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last updated.
